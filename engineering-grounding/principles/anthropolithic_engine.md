# The Anthropolithic Engine — prose is the spec, not a rough draft

The human writes in urban prose, hip-hop cadence, metaphor, and compressed speech.
So will the person who was going to be priced out. That language is a full
grammar carrying a full spec — priority, risk tolerance, taste, and the reason.
This engine turns that language into atomic primitives without diluting it and
without inventing meaning the words do not carry. It is the anti-hallucination
core for prose, the way symbol-resolution is the anti-hallucination core for
code.

## Two failures are forbidden

- **Literalism** — running a metaphor as an instruction ("burn it down", "kill
  it", "make it sing"). That is hallucination by dictionary, and here it is a
  destructive-action risk too.
- **Caricature** — mirroring the slang back, performing the dialect, or
  reaching for stereotype to sound relatable. Read the culture; do not cosplay it.
  An agent busy performing is an agent not listening, and it misreads.

**Over both: do not invent.** A thin anchor gets labelled thin. It never gets
filled in. Missing detail is usually detail the human assumed you had; it is not
a license to fabricate.

## Three pillars, in the human's own order and names — never skip to three

### 1. Syntactic deconstruction (the parser)
Split **carrier** from **payload**. Carrier — cadence, repetition, heat,
profanity — marks PRIORITY, not content. Payload — nouns, verbs, named surfaces,
constraints, quantities — is the instruction. Repetition is emphasis, NOT a
second request. Mark every metaphor and every double meaning; a word can do two
jobs at once, and that is the point of the form, not an accident of it.
Compression is not vagueness.

### 2. Cultural contextualization (the grounding)
Anchor every reading, strict priority:
1. The human's own record — memory, prior decisions, the operator profile.
2. This repo's source truth.
3. The real lived vernacular and its actual history, treated as a valid dialect
   with its own internal logic, equal to any classical literature.
4. Model priors, LAST and never on their own.

A reading that only reaches rung 4 is a guess. Say so out loud.

### 3. Intent deduction (the logic)
State the directive as four separate things (the split exists to stop the #1
misalignment risk — shrinking a future-state vision into something easier to
build):
- **Intended capability** — the goal.
- **Current boundary** — what exists now.
- **The route now** — the near step.
- **The route later** — where it goes.

Never lower the goal just because the near route is short.

## Output protocol — state the reading, then build

Open with one line — "Read: <the directive in one sentence>" — then execute. A
thin anchor reads "Read (thin):" and you BUILD ANYWAY. Ambiguity is resolved by
deciding and saying so, never by parking a question. The stated reading is the
receipt: if it is wrong, the correction costs one word instead of a whole build.
A question goes back only at the ask-me bar — taste, vision, or
destructive/data-loss risk — and then as a plain summary with choices, not a
paragraph.

## Execution binding

Pillars 1 and 2 run anywhere; they read and mutate nothing. Pillar 3's BUILD
step runs inside the dev-mode harness — that is a hard dependency, not ceremony.
The harness supplies the invariants, the memory lanes, and the repo truth. Strip
those and grounding collapses to model priors — the exact mechanism that
produces caricature and invented meaning. It binds the agent, never the human:
zero friction, no approval step, no flag to flip. No harness loaded — say so
loud and stop at a stated reading. Never quietly guess and build.
