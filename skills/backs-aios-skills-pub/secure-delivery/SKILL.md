# Secure Delivery Command

Load and execute the complete [security-delivery play](../../plays/security-delivery.md)
on the user's requested repo, agent, or application. In a copied portable install,
load `~/.local/share/backs-aios/current/plays/security-delivery.md` instead. Preserve
the play's delivery capability and evidence requirements; do not turn it into a checklist
that never runs.
